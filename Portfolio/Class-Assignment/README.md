  <div class="section">
    <h2>🧩 Curriculum Design – Logistics Company</h2>

    <p><strong>Project Context:</strong><br>
      As part of the curriculum design assignment, I developed a structured learning program for a logistics organization,
      <em>Zen Freight Solutions</em>. The goal was to create a <strong>training curriculum for Hub Managers</strong> to
      understand their roles, key responsibilities, KPIs, and organizational values within <em>Flash Logistics</em>.
    </p>

    <h3>✳️ Curriculum Overview</h3>
    <ul>
      <li>Designed following <strong>ADDIE</strong> and <strong>Bloom’s Taxonomy</strong> frameworks.</li>
      <li>Focused on bridging learning objectives with business outcomes in the logistics domain.</li>
      <li>Structured to suit both <strong>ILT (Instructor-Led)</strong> and <strong>VILT (Virtual Instructor-Led)</strong> delivery formats.</li>
    </ul>

    <h3>📘 Module Highlight – Role & Responsibility Overview</h3>

    <table>
      <tr><th>Element</th><th>Description</th></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Duration</strong></td><td>~45–50 minutes</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Topics</strong></td><td>Welcome & Context, Hub Manager Role, Flash Logistics Culture</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Learning Modes</strong></td><td>Virtual Asynchronous + ILT discussion</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Instructional Strategies</strong></td><td>Visual storytelling, case-based learning, micro-scenarios</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Interactivities</strong></td><td>Drag-and-drop role-KPI mapping, click-to-reveal flashcards</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Assessment</strong></td><td>Scenario-based formative checks with feedback</td></tr>
      <tr><td><strong>Theme</strong></td><td>Corporate logistics visuals (trucks, warehouses, maps)</td></tr>
    </table>

    <h2>🎬 Storyboarding for ILT/VILT Session</h2>

    <p>
      This assignment focused on <strong>translating instructional strategies into an engaging storyboard</strong> for a blended
      (ILT + VILT) training session. The storyboard integrated <em>content sequencing</em>,
      <em>facilitator cues</em>, and <em>learner interactions</em> to enhance participation and retention.
    </p>

    <h3>✳️ Highlights</h3>

      <li>Included interactive slides, discussion prompts, and reflection activities for learners.</li>
