<h1 style="color:#1e3a8a; text-align:center;">Generative AI for School and Beyond</h1>

<h2 style="color:#1e3a8a; text-align:center;">Interactive eLearning | Rise 360 | Gen AI Literacy & Ethics</h2>


<p style="color:#2563eb; font-size:17px;">
This interactive <b>web-based training (WBT)</b> introduces learners to the fascinating world of Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI). 
Built in <b>Articulate Rise 360</b>, the 30–40 minute self-paced course simplifies complex AI concepts through relatable analogies, 
hands-on interactivities, and guided reflection — making AI accessible to both students and educators.
</p>


<hr style="border:1px solid #dce1eb; margin:25px 0;">

<h2 style="color:#1e3a8a;">Learning Objectives</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Explain what Generative AI is and how it differs from traditional AI.</li>
  <li>Describe how GenAI models (GANs, VAEs, Transformers, Diffusion) work.</li>
  <li>Identify and explore real-world GenAI tools across text, image, music, video, and code.</li>
  <li>Apply responsible and ethical AI practices in educational and creative contexts.</li>
  <li>Earn a completion certificate and badge upon assessment.</li>
</ul>

<hr style="border:1px solid #dce1eb; margin:25px 0;">


<h2 style="color:#1e3a8a;">Key Features</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Modular design:</b> Four short modules with interactive activities and formative quizzes.</li>
  <li><b>Scenario-based learning:</b> Real-world use cases and reflection prompts to promote ethical thinking.</li>
  <li><b>Engaging visuals:</b> Labeled graphics, flashcards, and accordions for concept clarity.</li>
  <li><b>Pedagogical framework:</b> Structured using <i>Gagné’s Nine Events of Instruction</i> for progressive learning.</li>
  <li><b>Gamified outcomes:</b> Learners unlock a certificate and badge upon successful completion.</li>
</ul>

<hr style="border:1px solid #dce1eb; margin:25px 0;">


<h2 style="color:#1e3a8a;">Course Structure</h2>
<ul>
  <li><b>Introduction –</b> Welcome and overview of GenAI and its relevance.</li>
  <li><b>Module 1: What is GenAI?</b> Understanding GenAI through analogies, examples, and comparisons.</li>
  <li><b>Module 2: How GenAI Works</b> Exploring the training–learning–generation workflow and model types.</li>
  <li><b>Module 3: Tools You Can Try</b> Experimenting with AI tools across multiple creative domains.</li>
  <li><b>Module 4: Ethics & Responsibility</b> Exploring AI ethics, fairness, and responsible use in schools.</li>
  <li><b>Final Quiz & Certification –</b> Reflect, assess, and earn your badge of completion.</li>
</ul>

<hr style="border:1px solid #dce1eb; margin:25px 0;">


<h2 style="color:#1e3a8a;">Instructional Design Highlights</h2>
<ul>
  <li>Follows <b>Gagné’s Nine Events</b> for attention, engagement, and feedback.</li>
  <li>Integrates <b>interactive learning elements</b> such as flashcards, matching pairs, and scenario-based reflection.</li>
  <li>Focuses on <b>AI literacy</b>, <b>critical thinking</b>, and <b>ethical digital citizenship</b>.</li>
  <li>Includes educational references from CBSE and <i>Sumita Arora’s Artificial Intelligence (Class IX)</i> textbook.</li>
</ul>

<hr style="border:1px solid #dce1eb; margin:25px 0;">

<h2 style="color:#1e3a8a;">Outcome</h2>
<p style="font-size:16px;">
Learners complete the course with a clear understanding of how Generative AI functions, 
its creative applications, and ethical responsibilities. The course encourages thoughtful, 
responsible use of AI tools — empowering students and educators to innovate with integrity.
</p>

<p style="font-size:16px; color:#64748b;">
<b>Certificate of Completion:</b> Issued automatically upon passing the final quiz.
</p>
