  <h1 style="color:#1e3a8a; text-align:center;">💼 Corporate Learning Project</h1>
  <h1 style="color:#1e3a8a; text-align:center;">Negotiation Mastery: Managing Employee Expectations</h1>

  <h2 style="color:#2563eb;">Project Overview</h2>
  <p>
    <b>Negotiation Mastery</b> is an interactive scenario-based eLearning module that simulates a
    high-stakes salary negotiation between an employee and her manager. Built in <strong>Rise 360</strong>,
    the course helps learners navigate emotionally nuanced conversations, practice ethical leadership,
    and apply empathetic communication strategies in the workplace.
  </p>

  <hr style="border:1px solid #e2e8f0; margin:20px 0;">

  <h2 style="color:#2563eb;">Learning Objectives</h2>
  <ul>
    <li>Practice empathetic leadership in sensitive workplace conversations</li>
    <li>Evaluate negotiation strategies and their impact on employee morale</li>
    <li>Make ethical decisions under pressure and reflect on outcomes</li>
    <li>Recognize the emotional consequences of managerial communication styles</li>
  </ul>

  <h2 style="color:#2563eb;">Key Features</h2>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>Three manager response paths:</strong> agreement, negotiation, and refusal</li>
    <li><strong>Learner-driven branching:</strong> follow-up decisions based on employee reactions</li>
    <li><strong>Narration via voiceover:</strong> smooth transitions between emotional moments</li>
    <li><strong>Text-based avatar dialogue:</strong> accessibility with emotional clarity</li>
    <li><strong>Modular design:</strong> structured for future Storyline integration</li>
  </ul>
